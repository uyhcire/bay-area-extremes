# Gaps, and the restricted data that would close them

| # | Gap | How it is handled now | Size (2024 unless noted) | Data that would close it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Non-provider items are not built from the income side (retail drugs, devices, insurer net cost, government administration, research, investment) | CMS expenditure values, decomposed with I-O coefficients | $1.33 trillion, 25% of the total | Census confidential microdata (Economic Census, Annual Retail/Wholesale Trade Surveys) for pharmacy and wholesaler margins; NAIC statutory filings by line of business and the CMS medical loss ratio files (public from 2011) for insurers; manufacturer net-price data (SSR Health, IQVIA) for rebates |
| 2 | Private-practice income by legal form | BEA residual surplus; proprietors' income only at the health-care-and-social-assistance level | Provider surplus $368 billion (private NAICS 62) | IRS SOI microdata (Forms 1065, 1120-S, Schedule C) by 4–6 digit NAICS, linked to W-2s to separate owner pay from profit |
| 3 | Labor share of owner-physician and other proprietor income | Assumed 67% (50–85%) | ±$24 billion between labor and capital | The same IRS linkages; Census LEHD/LBD owner files |
| 4 | Surplus of nonprofit providers other than hospitals (nursing homes, clinics, home health, dialysis) | Not added | Not quantified | IRS Form 990 e-file microdata by NTEE/NAICS; Medicare cost reports for skilled nursing facilities, home health agencies and renal facilities (public, not yet processed here) |
| 5 | Uniformed military medical personnel; split of VA and DoD spending between own facilities and purchased care | Federal providers built from civilian federal payroll with an assumed output ratio | Federal provider output $114 billion, range −$22 to +$37 billion | DoD Defense Health Program and VA medical-care budget execution data by object class (public budget appendices would narrow it; Defense Manpower data would close it) |
| 6 | Profit of drug and device makers booked abroad | Appears as imports at the border; not traced to foreign labor and capital | Imports $410 billion, 7.8% of the total | OECD ICIO tables (blocked from this environment); BEA multinational enterprise microdata; IRS Form 5471/8975 country-by-country reports |
| 7 | Import content at industry level | BEA import-proportionality assumption | Affects the imports/domestic split only | Census linked trade-transaction and establishment microdata (LFTTD) |
| 8 | Medicaid supplemental payments and provider taxes | Not separable; provider taxes are inside taxes on production | Not quantified | CMS-64 expenditure reports and state directed-payment preprints (public but unstructured); T-MSIS |
| 9 | Home and community services inside the NHE boundary | 85% (70–100%) of NAICS 62412 output | ±$19 billion | T-MSIS claims by provider taxonomy; CMS's internal NHEA worksheets for "other health, residential, and personal care" |
| 10 | State/local health function detail | BEA aggregate; 2017 input mix held fixed | Output $480 billion | Census of Governments individual-unit files (public) by function and object |
| 11 | Hospital financials quality | As-filed Medicare cost reports with outlier rules | Net income sensitive by $7–11 billion a year | Audited financial statements (EMMA/Definitive Healthcare/AHA Annual Survey, licensed) |
| 12 | I-O tables stop at 2023 and predate the 2026 industry revision | 2023 coefficients for 2024; older vintage for coefficients | Affects Version B shares only | Next BEA I-O release |
| 13 | Rebates, by channel | CMS nets rebates from retail drugs; margin shares come from gross sales | Rebates were about $108 billion in 2018 per the BEA–CMS reconciliation | Manufacturer and PBM contract data; CMS Part D direct and indirect remuneration files |
| 14 | 1997 and 2025 | 1997 not produced (NIPA income tables start 1998); 2025 provider block only | — | CMS NHEA 2025 (expected within months) |
| 15 | Sources unreachable from this environment | FRED, `download.bls.gov`, BLS productivity tables, OECD | — | Network access or API keys; none change the estimate |
